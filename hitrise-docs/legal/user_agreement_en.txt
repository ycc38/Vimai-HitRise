3. Usage Requirements
Use HitRise in a safe, open, and suitable exercise environment. Before training, check that the device is installed securely, the cord and surrounding area are safe, and your training intensity matches your physical condition.

If you experience illness, injury, fatigue, dizziness, chest discomfort, or any other discomfort, stop training immediately and seek professional advice if needed. Minors should use the app under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

4. Bluetooth and Training Data
The app receives SENBALL# device data through Bluetooth and displays punch count, Relative Power Score, battery, and round statistics. Data may vary due to device status, sensor tolerance, Bluetooth signal, usage posture, and environment.

Relative Power Score is the raw sensor value reported by compatible hardware. It has not been calibrated against standard force-measurement equipment, is unitless, and must not be interpreted as kilograms (kg), kilogram-force (kgf), or newtons (N). It is intended only for relative comparison using the same device under similar conditions and must not be used as an actual force measurement, product force certification, or official competition result.

HitRise training data is used for exercise feedback, statistics, achievements, and leaderboards. It should not be used as medical diagnosis, rehabilitation assessment, nutrition advice, or official competition judging.
