HitRise support

Request deletion of HitRise data

HitRise is operated by Shaoxing Weimai Technology Co., Ltd. The app creates a local identity automatically; there is no username and password account to close.

How to request deletion

  1. Email zclei@vip.sina.com with the subject HitRise data deletion request.
  2. Include your HitRise nickname and, if available, your in-app user ID or sync code so we can identify the correct records. Do not send a password.
  3. We may ask for information needed to verify that the records belong to you before deleting them.

What is deleted

Once verified, we will delete the cloud profile and training data associated with your HitRise identity within 30 days. This includes training history, training summaries, achievements, leaderboard entries, nickname, avatar, and related profile information. If a record cannot be deleted or must be retained under applicable law, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period in our response.

Uninstalling HitRise removes local app data according to your device's operating system. Uninstalling alone does not request deletion of cloud data; use the email process above for cloud records.

Some security or legal records may be retained only for the period required by applicable law. We do not keep other HitRise cloud profile and training data after completing a verified deletion request.

For more information, see the HitRise Privacy Policy.
